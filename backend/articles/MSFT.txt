Microsoft Corporation (MSFT) announced Q2 revenue of $62.0 billion, an increase of 18% driven by Microsoft Cloud revenue of $33.7 billion, up 24%. Azure and other cloud services revenue grew 30%, with 6 percentage points of growth directly attributed to AI services. Microsoft Copilot adoption expanded rapidly across 40% of Fortune 100 companies. Productivity and Business Processes revenue reached $19.2 billion, up 13%. More Personal Computing rose 19% to $16.9 billion. CEO Satya Nadella affirmed that Microsoft is applying AI at scale across every layer of the tech stack. Key risks include escalating AI infrastructure capex, data center power constraints, hardware supply limitations, and intensifying cybersecurity threats.
