NVIDIA Corporation (NVDA) reported record Q4 revenue of $22.1 billion, up 265% from a year ago. Growth was driven by massive enterprise demand for H100 Tensor Core GPUs and Hopper architecture. Data Center revenue grew 409% year-over-year to $18.4 billion. Full year revenue reached $60.9 billion, up 126%. CEO Jensen Huang highlighted that accelerated computing and generative AI have reached the tipping point, with surging demand across companies, industries, and nations. Gross margin expanded to 76.0%. Key risks include export control restrictions on sales to China, supply chain bottlenecks with advanced packaging (CoWoS), and potential customer concentration as top cloud service providers ramp internal ASIC chip designs.
